# AI Tutor Workflow

Quick Maths is the source of progress data. The AI tutor is the practice partner, diagnostician, and reviewer.

Use this workflow:

1. Take a Quick Maths mastery test.
2. Complete the reflection before saving.
3. Open `Exports`.
4. Copy the Tutor Setup Prompt.
5. Copy or download `latest_tutor_summary.md`.
6. Paste both into your preferred AI tutor.
7. Practice one weak point at a time.
8. Retest the recommended Quick Maths skill when ready.

If the Results page creates a pending review packet:

1. Copy `latest_review_packet.md`.
2. Paste it into the tutor with the setup prompt.
3. Ask the tutor to review each proof obligation or rubric criterion.
4. Enter the tutor's verdict, score, confidence, obligation statuses, rubric points, and feedback back into Quick Maths.
5. Retest or revise based on the review.

The tutor should not replace Quick Maths mastery tracking. It should use Quick Maths evidence to decide what to teach next, ask one practice question at a time, review final answers and work separately, and tell you when to retest.
