## Compatibility

Known legacy categories are converted when lesson sets, curricula, backups and Studio drafts load. The original category becomes the topic. Unknown custom branches are kept. Reuse the broad branches above for new lessons and reserve topics for smaller teaching units. New fields and branches remain supported.

The machine-readable source, including legacy aliases, is [learning-taxonomy.json](./learning-taxonomy.json). This reference is generated by `scripts/build_branch_reference.py`.
